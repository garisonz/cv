VeriStox

Problem

As a long-time investor, I often struggled to find trustworthy, unbiased data. While top investors rely heavily on SEC filings for their decisions, I found the official interface overwhelming—difficult to navigate, full of jargon, and not beginner-friendly.

Solution

I built VeriStox—a clean, intuitive platform that makes SEC filings accessible and understandable. By simplifying complex disclosures and eliminating bias, VeriStox empowers investors of all levels to make informed decisions with confidence.

Nexa

Problem

Modern knowledge management tools are powerful, but they can be overly complex, proprietary, or limited in customization. I wanted a streamlined, self-hosted alternative that I could fully control and tailor to my workflow.

Solution

I built a Notion-style web app that lets users create, edit, and organize notes on a dynamic, single-page dashboard. Inspired by Notion’s flexibility, my clone supports nested pages, markdown-style formatting, drag-and-drop reordering, and persistent storage.

Data Warehouse

Problem

As data volume evolves, it becomes increasingly difficult to generate unified insights. Analysts spent too much time manually cleaning and combining data, leading to inconsistent reporting and delayed decisions.

Solution

I built a centralized Data Warehouse that consolidates structured data from multiple sources into a single, query-optimized environment. This streamlined data analysis, improved reporting consistency, and enabled scalable business intelligence.

Data Pipeline

Problem

Businesses often rely on batch data processing, which creates delays in responding to critical events such as user behavior changes, fraud detection, or system anomalies. There was a need for a system that could process and analyze data in real time.

Solution

I built a Real-Time Data Pipeline that ingests, processes, and delivers data with minimal latency—enabling real-time analytics and alerts. The system handles high-throughput data streams and supports scalable, event-driven architecture.

Fintech Advisor

Problem

Many banking customers struggle to understand their financial health or make informed decisions due to overwhelming jargon and fragmented tools. Traditional banking apps lack intelligent, conversational interfaces that offer tailored financial guidance in real time.

Solution

I developed Fintech Advisor, a virtual banking assistant that helps users manage their finances through intelligent, conversational interactions. Powered by the Gemini API, the assistant provides personalized financial insights, budgeting tips, and transaction analysis in a user-friendly chat interface.

Sentiment Analysis

Problem

Understanding public opinion at scale—especially across social media, product reviews, or customer feedback—is challenging without automated tools. Manually analyzing thousands of texts is inefficient and prone to bias.

Solution

I developed a Sentiment Analysis system that automatically classifies text as positive, negative, or neutral. It enables businesses to gauge public sentiment, monitor brand perception, and respond to feedback in real time.
